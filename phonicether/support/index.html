Contact us

Send a problem report

  • iPhone or iPad: open Settings in the app, then choose Report a problem.
  • Mac: open the options menu, then choose Report a problem.

Describe what happened, then share the report with us. The app does not upload it by itself. Our Privacy Policy describes what a report contains.

Subscriptions

Apple handles payments, renewals, cancellations and refunds. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.
