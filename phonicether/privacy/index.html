PhonicEther Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

The short version

  • PhonicEther sends audio from your Mac to the iPhones and iPads you pair. It has no sign-in or user account.
  • Your audio and track information do not go to us. We do not run a server that receives them.
  • The app does not record or save your audio.
  • We receive diagnostic data only if you choose to send us a support report.
  • The apps have no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers the PhonicEther apps for Mac, iPhone and iPad. “We” means the developer of PhonicEther, shown as the seller on the App Store page. Other services you use with PhonicEther, such as Apple, Spotify and your email provider, have their own policies.

Device permissions

  • Screen and system audio recording (Mac): to capture the audio you choose to send.
  • Automation (Mac): to read track information from the app you select. Audio sharing works without it.
  • Local network: to find your devices and send audio between them.
  • Bluetooth: to find your Mac or iPhone when a VPN blocks Wi-Fi discovery.
  • Camera (iPhone and iPad): to scan the pairing QR code. The app reads the code and does not save camera images.
  • Notifications (iPhone and iPad): for reminders set on your device, such as a reminder before a free trial ends. We do not send push notifications.
  • Photos or Files (iPhone and iPad): only when you choose a screenshot for a support report.

3. Support reports

You can create a report with Report a problem in the app. The app does not upload a report by itself. You review the report, then choose where to share or save it. For example, you can email it to us.

A report can contain:

  • the problem type, the time and the description that you enter;
  • recent app logs: app activity, connection changes, errors, and technical details about audio and connection quality;
  • the app version, operating system, device model and time zone, and on a Mac, the free disk space;
  • a screenshot, if you add one;
  • Apple crash and performance reports, if you include them.

Logs do not contain your audio. They can contain device identifiers, the names of paired devices and the name of the audio source app. The app tries to remove passwords, keys, web addresses, IP addresses, email addresses and user names in file paths. Automatic filtering can miss something, so check your description and screenshot before you share them.

If you send us a report or email, we also receive your email address and anything else in your message. We use this data only to investigate the problem, fix bugs, improve reliability and reply to you.

4. Who else can receive data

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. The following parties can receive data when you use PhonicEther:

  • Apple: processes App Store purchases and subscriptions. If you agree in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple can also give us crash and usage statistics.
  • Google: stores the email messages and reports you send to our Gmail address.
  • Spotify: when Spotify is your source and track sharing is on, your Mac downloads cover art directly from Spotify’s image servers. Spotify can see your Mac’s IP address. The request does not use cookies.
  • GitHub: hosts this web page and can log the technical details of your visit.
  • Legal requests: we can disclose data if the law requires it.

Any third party that we allow to receive user data must protect it at a level equal to this policy. The current apps contain no third-party analytics or advertising code. If we plan to add a third-party analytics tool, we will update this policy first and ask for your permission where the law or App Store rules require it.

5. How long data is kept

  • Mac logs: up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • iPhone and iPad logs: up to a fixed size limit. The app removes older entries as new ones arrive.
  • Unsent report drafts: 7 days.
  • Reports and emails we receive: until the problem is resolved, and no longer than 12 months, unless the law requires us to keep them or you ask us to keep helping you.
  • Everything else on your devices: until you remove it or delete the app. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted.

6. Your choices

  • Track information: on your Mac, turn off Share track information in the audio source controls. It is on until you turn it off.
  • Permissions: change them at any time in Settings on your iPhone or iPad, or in System Settings on your Mac.
  • Paired devices: use Revoke or Reset pairing in the Mac app.
  • Diagnostics on iPhone or iPad: open Settings in the app, then Saved diagnostics, then Clear all diagnostics.
  • Diagnostics on Mac: in the options menu, choose Diagnostics, then Show logs in Finder, and delete the files.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel it in your Apple account settings.
  • Reports you sent us: contact us to withdraw your consent and ask us to delete them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. We hold your personal data only if you contacted us or sent us a report. Contact us to use these rights. We reply within 30 days, and we will not treat you differently for making a request. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent when you send a report, and on our legitimate interest in answering you and keeping the app reliable.

8. Security

PhonicEther uses a pairing step to limit who can receive your audio. It encrypts audio and track information with AES-256 and authenticates them with HMAC-SHA-256. Pairing keys are stored in the Keychain. The encryption specification describes the full format. No system is completely secure, so keep your pairing QR code and guest PIN private. People you pair as guests can hear your audio and see track information.

9. International transfers

Email and reports you send us are stored by our email provider and can be processed in countries other than your own, which can have different data protection laws.

10. Children

PhonicEther is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page and update the effective date. If a change has a significant effect on how we use your data, we will also tell you in the app or in the App Store release notes before it takes effect.
